About AeroNotes

A student blog on aerospace & space.

Hi, I'm Umutcan Bakırcı. I study aerospace engineering, and AeroNotes is where I write down what I learn — from wingtip aerodynamics and jet propulsion to launch vehicles and satellites.

The goal is simple: explain complex aerospace topics clearly, with enough depth to be useful for fellow students and enthusiasts, without drowning in jargon.

What you'll find here

  • Aviation — how aircraft fly and how they are getting more efficient
  • World News — the latest aviation and space news from around the world
  • Space — launch vehicles, satellites and mission design
  • Engines & Sustainability — jet and rocket engines, fuels and the road to cleaner flight
  • Technology — materials, avionics and tools of the trade
