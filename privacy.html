Privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

AeroNotes is a personal, non-commercial blog run by Umutcan Bakırcı. It is built to collect as little data as possible.

What we do not do

  • No cookies, no advertising and no tracking or analytics scripts of our own. (The X box on the home page is the one exception, see below.)
  • No accounts, sign-ups or newsletters, so no email addresses are collected.
  • The site's main font and its images are served from this site itself, not from third parties.

Comments

If you leave a comment, we store the name you type, your comment and the time it was sent. To stop spam, we also store a one-way hash of your IP address, which cannot be turned back into the address. Comments are published right away. Comments with offensive words are refused automatically, comments containing links are checked by the author first, and the author can remove any comment.

This data is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts the site. You can ask for your comment to be removed at any time by sending a message on LinkedIn.

Quiz and flashcards

Your quiz progress and best score are saved only in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device, and you can clear them by clearing your browser data.

Links and embeds

Share buttons open LinkedIn, X, Facebook or WhatsApp only when you click them. Some articles may embed YouTube videos (privacy-enhanced mode) or use extra fonts from Google Fonts. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Posts from X on the home page

The "Aerospace Live" box on the home page shows headlines from public news feeds of aerospace agencies and companies. Our server collects these headlines once an hour, so your browser does not contact those sites until you click a headline. The headlines and articles belong to their publishers.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical request data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site.
